Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. When it happens in New Britain, New Britain Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Frequently Asked Questions About Sewage Cleanup

A blocked sewer line on the property is the most common starting point. Roots from nearby trees, flushed wipes and accumulated grease account for a large share of those clogs. Older lines can also crack or collapse, and heavy rain can overload the municipal sewer and push sewage back through floor drains. To know for certain, have the line camera-inspected once cleanup is finished.
Some things can be kept and others cannot. Glass, sealed tile, metal and other hard surfaces that do not absorb water are normally salvageable after disinfection. Porous materials that contacted sewage, including carpet, padding, drywall, insulation, mattresses and upholstered furniture, have to be discarded. For insurance purposes, our crew keeps a list of everything that had to be removed.
The emergency phase, meaning sewage removal plus decontamination, generally lasts 1 to 3 days. If materials have to be replaced, full restoration can stretch to 1 to 3 weeks, based on how much was damaged. Throughout, the focus is on returning your home to a safe, livable state without delay.
Sewage cleanup costs in New Britain typically range from $2,000 to $10,000 or more, depending on the extent of contamination and the area affected. Expect roughly $1,500 to $3,000 when the backup is minor and contained, and more than $15,000 in cases of extensive flooding that need materials replaced and rebuilding done.
Standard homeowner's insurance often does NOT cover sewage backup unless you have a specific sewage or water backup endorsement. We strongly recommend adding this coverage, which is typically inexpensive ($50 to $100 per year) but provides critical protection. Check your policy or ask your agent.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Do not wait on a slow drain, which can turn into a full blockage
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in New Britain

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • More than one drain in the house overflowing or backing up together
  • Lower levels or drain openings giving off a heavy sewage odor, sometimes like rotten eggs
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste
