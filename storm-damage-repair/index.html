How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor

Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
