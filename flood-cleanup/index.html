A sump pump that quits, a sewer that backs up, a pipe that lets go, rising river water, storm surge or a hard rain can each flood a property, and the damage should be handled by professionals without delay. Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. New Britain Restoration handles emergency flood cleanup across New Britain and answers 24/7 to pump out the water, sanitize what it touched and restore your property safely.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Add downspout extensions so roof water empties well away from the foundation
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • Keep documents and keepsakes in watertight bins on shelves, not on a basement floor

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Nothing else reliably heads off secondary damage, and that damage can run thousands more to remediate, so we dry by the numbers.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Cracked foundation walls, low doorways or window wells letting water inside
  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Living areas flooded by contaminated water from a backed-up drain or sewer
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Tide marks on drywall or trim where the water peaked
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
