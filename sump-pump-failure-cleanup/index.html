Dealing With Sump Pump Failure Cleanup in New Britain

A sump pump has one job, and it tends to fail at the moment it is needed most. During heavy rain the water table climbs and fills the pit, at which point an outage, a stuck float or an overwhelmed pump stops the pumping. The pit overflows and the basement floor starts to flood. It keeps coming until a pump is running again.

Do not think of it as clean water. It has come through soil and often carries silt, bacteria and whatever was in the drain tile, so we treat it as Category 2 at best and Category 3 if it has sat or mixed with sewage. For the job, this means soaked porous items such as drywall and carpet pad are usually discarded, and what stays gets washed and disinfected first, then dried.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Stay Out of the WaterWith the electricity still on, a flooded basement is not safe to walk into. Outlets, the pump cord or the furnace might already be wet and live.
  2. Shut Off Basement PowerBasement breakers should be flipped off, provided you can get to the panel without standing in water. A panel surrounded by water is a job for the power company.
  3. Check the Pump SafelySee if the plug pulled out, the outlet has tripped or the float got stuck on the side of the pit. Do not reach into the water.
  4. Treat the Water as ContaminatedChildren and pets stay on the upper floor, anyone who has to enter wears rubber boots and gloves, and hands get washed after contact with anything wet.
  5. Save What Is Dry, Then CallCarry dry valuables upstairs, photograph the water level and the pump, then call us. Holding off until the basement empties is not the answer.
Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Get the Water Out

Our crew arrives with extractors and pumps, which means your dead sump is not needed. Low spots, the area by the pit and the rest of the floor are cleared of water and silt.

2

Remove Contaminated Materials

Our team wears protective gear to remove wet drywall, insulation, carpet and pad reached by groundwater, then bags and hauls it off, leaving bare framing and concrete.

3

Wash and Disinfect

An antimicrobial product goes on after the concrete, the studs and any kept belongings have been washed. Doing it ahead of drying keeps the air movers from spreading contaminants through the basement.

4

Dry and Verify

We run drying equipment until our meters show normal moisture in the slab and the wood, which typically takes 3 to 5 days. A working pump should be back in the pit before our equipment leaves.

Why It Happens

How It Usually Starts

  • Undersized equipment that falls behind when inflow is heavy.
  • A float switch that is stuck, tangled or pinned against the basin wall.
  • Check valve trouble, which makes the pump move the same water again and again.
  • Outside the house, a crushed, clogged or separated discharge pipe.
  • An intake screen packed with mud and stones so the pump cannot draw water.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Bottom Plates and Studs

Framing lumber at floor level takes on groundwater before anything else in the wall. We clean, disinfect and dry the studs and plates after removing the wet drywall.

Boxes and Stored Items

Books, cloth, boxes and wood furniture legs take in dirty water within a short time. Porous items left sitting in the water resist disinfecting and grow mold in a matter of days.

Drywall and Wall Insulation

Drywall and insulation wick dirty water upward from the slab, well above the visible water line. The lower part that got wet is cut away, not dried, on account of the contamination.

Outlets and Wiring

Basement outlets sit low on the wall, where rising water can reach them. Any outlet or wiring that got wet should be checked by an electrician before the circuit is turned back on.

Will Insurance Pay?

Insurance and Sump Pump Failure Cleanup

Sump pump losses are widely misunderstood when it comes to insurance. A standard homeowners policy usually excludes water that backs up or overflows from a sump, and flood insurance is a separate policy with its own rules. Coverage often exists only if you added a water backup or sump overflow endorsement, and those carry their own limits. Your declarations page will list it, or your agent can tell you. Our records of the failed pump, how high the water rose and what it damaged go into your claim file.
