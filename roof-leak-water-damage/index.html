Roof Leak Water Damage Help in New Britain

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Our team maps everything that got wet, then dries it.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesIf water is dripping through a light fixture, a ceiling fan or near an outlet, switch off that circuit at the breaker and leave the fixture alone.
  2. Contain the WaterFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
Common Causes

What Causes It

  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Water backing up at the eave and under the shingles because the gutters are blocked.
  • Wind-torn, curling or split shingles that leave the underlayment open to rain.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Gaps at chimneys, vents and sidewalls where the flashing has separated or rusted through.
Why Speed Matters

Where the Damage Spreads

Wall Cavities

From the attic, a leak can drop into a stud bay and soak the wall insulation and drywall from behind. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

Mold in the Attic

Mold does well in an attic that is warm, dark and full of damp wood and drywall paper. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Roof Framing

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Ceiling Drywall

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Our Process

How We Handle Roof Leak Water Damage

1

Map Where Water Went

Moisture meters and a thermal camera let us follow the rain through the attic, the ceilings and the walls, so spots that look dry from underneath are not missed.

2

Tarp and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. We also cover floors and contents under the damaged ceiling.

3

Tear Out and Dry

We pull out insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, then run air movers and dehumidifiers in the attic and the rooms below, checking moisture readings until the framing is dry.

4

Sanitize and Repair

An antimicrobial product goes on any open wood that needs it. With the roof fixed, our team installs insulation and drywall, finishes the joints, and primes and paints so the repair blends in.

Coverage

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. A leak put down to an old, worn or neglected roof is usually denied, and fixing the roof may be treated separately from the interior work. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.
